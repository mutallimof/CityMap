Ferry timetables

Every line on the map that says “Official timetable” or “<operator> timetable” runs on the departure times its operator publishes. We keep the facts only (pier, day type, departure time) — no texts, prices, images or logos — and draw the boats with the map’s own vapur. Arrival times are not printed; the map computes them from the route. Lines whose timetable doesn’t cover a date are labelled “Simulated schedule”.

    S.S. Turizm ve Yolcu Deniz Taşıyıcılar Kooperatifi. The site prints no validity period and no holiday timetable: a read counts for 45 days, holidays show the simulated schedule. Where one boat is printed to two destinations, the order of calls is our reading (†).
  • Dentur Avrasya — “Üsküdar - Kabataş Sefer Saatleri” on denturavrasya.com, read once a week.
    The site prints no validity period and no holiday timetable for this line: a read counts for 45 days, holidays show the simulated schedule. “Sürekli sefer” (continuous service) windows are shown as a note, without made-up times.

Times can change without notice (the operators say so); check the operator before you travel.
